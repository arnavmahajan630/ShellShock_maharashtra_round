# B1 — 16 main problems and the checker

## Built

- `ml/problems/main/`: P01–P14, P16, P17 (P15 is Strong and is not here). Held out: P04, P09, P13.
- `ml/problems/check.py`: schema, variant and test rules, class coverage, then every correct variant on gcc and on the interpreter. `dsa` uses the same checker.
- `tests/B1/test_main_bank.py`: the rules that do not run code.

P03 and P11 started from the W0 fixtures. P11 gained an adversarial tag on the test where the code is already 42. The fixtures themselves were not edited.

## Decisions

1. **M04 has one training problem.** 03 §3.3.1 puts it on P07 (train) and P13 (holdout) only. The checker requires every other main class on at least two training problems, and requires M04 on those two ids. Q05 also lists M04, which is the DSA bank's business.
2. **Exposure is 0.7 or 0.4.** The first targets in the 03 row are 0.7, the rest 0.4. P03 and P11 keep the fixture's numbers.
3. **Branch problems do not grow a fake loop.** P11, P12, P13, P14, P16 and P17 have three correct styles (if, flag or early return, ternary). Loop families must contain both `for` and `while` somewhere in the variants.
4. **`allowed_ops` always includes `oth_*`**, plus `amb_*` where the row is an M01/M08 twin surface. Globs match the classes in `exposure`.
5. **P16 exposes OTHER.** That is the chained-comparison problem. OTHER is a label, not one of the 17 ids, so the checker allows it as an exposure key.
6. **An adversarial test may also be a sample.** The two flags answer different questions.
