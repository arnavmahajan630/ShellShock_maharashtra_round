## What a star means

No model is trained here. Every number is computed from the knowledge table (P per misconception, written by the diagnoser, the quiz, the sentence reader and the exam) and the exposure `e_k` each bank problem states.

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `cleared` | An attempt with every test passed, or a node with status `done`, for one of the skill's record ids. A later failed attempt does not un-clear it. |
| `state` | `uncharted`; `unexplored`; `attempted` (tried, not cleared); `known` (cleared, no linked misconception in ACTIVE / TREATING / PROBATION / RELAPSED); `shaky` (cleared, one is). |
| `brightness` | Cleared stars: `Π (1 − e_k · P_k)`. A blank learner's cleared star is about 0.83–0.87, not 1.0, because nothing has been ruled out yet. |
| `risk` | Uncleared stars: `1 − Π (1 − e_k · P_k)`. |
| `at_risk` | Uncleared, and a linked misconception has `P ≥ 0.5` and `e_k · P_k ≥ 0.35`. A blank learner has no halos. |
| `suggested_next` | The playable, uncleared skill with the largest `sum(class_eigs(P, exposure))` (the exam's score). It is the most informative next step, not a teaching order. |

## Decisions

1. **A skill is a problem family.** The 34 bank families are all distinct, so one bank problem per star. Exposure and problem names are read from the bank at load, not copied into the chart file; `check` fails if a family is missing or used twice.
2. **P13 and P14.** The app serves different problems under these ids (`sync_ratio`, `signal_diff`) than the bank does (`fuel_percent`, `distance`). Any stored P13/P14 record comes from the app, so the app problems are their own stars and the bank skills `ratio` and `return_value` have `"record_ids": []`: they can show risk but cannot be cleared from stored records.
3. **Eight app-only stars** (P13, P14, P18–P23) have no linked misconception: they can be lit, never shaky or at risk. Their attempts are saved with `record_attempt` + `set_node` only, so no P moves from a rule-based finding.
4. **Trials** light an exam star through `also_cleared_by` (T1 → `two_pointer_scan`, T2 → `binary_search`, T3 → `bubble_sort`, T4 → `string_two_pointer`, T5 → `rec_product`). Only the node is written. The star can still be shaky if the model-side P for a linked misconception is high.
5. **Saving in `/attempt`.** `Store.apply_attempt_result` is called after the diagnosis is built; the learner prior of that diagnosis was read before the write. An unknown learner writes nothing and the database file is not created. A probe answer that makes the diagnosis confident saves once (flag on the remembered attempt); the attempt row is replaced, not duplicated.
6. **Halos need a strong link.** With `P ≥ 0.5` alone, one diagnosed mistake put a halo on every skill that lists it, including exposure 0.4: 13 stars after one mistake and 23 after two. With `e_k · P_k ≥ 0.35` it is the skills where that mistake is a main exposure.
7. **Layout.** A topic has a centre and a radius; its skills sit evenly on that ring in file order, first at the top. Only uncharted stars carry their own position. The screen pushes each skill name away from its ring's centre.
8. **The outline of C** (uncharted list and prereq lines) is ours. It is not taken from a standard or a textbook.
